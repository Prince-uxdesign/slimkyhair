Shipping & Delivery

Here's what to expect when ordering Slimky Hair products.

Transparent Two-Step Fulfillment: To ensure fair and accurate courier rates based on destination and weight, product checkout and delivery fees are processed separately. You pay for your formulations first, and your delivery fee is quoted and paid separately prior to dispatch.

Important Information

Essential guidelines regarding your order fulfillment and delivery experience.

Shipping Fees

Product prices at checkout do not bundle arbitrary shipping markups. Delivery fees reflect the actual courier charges required to transport your items safely to your chosen destination.

Delivery Arrangements

Fulfillment and delivery arrangements are coordinated directly between our dispatch team and the customer via our designated delivery provider / delivery service to ensure optimal timing and handling.

International Shipping

Worldwide orders are handled on a bespoke basis. A custom shipping quote is prepared following product purchase and must be accepted and paid before dispatch begins.

Customs & Import Charges

Cross-border shipments may be evaluated by local destination customs authorities. Any resulting import fees or assessments are separate from initial carriage costs.

Address Accuracy

Please ensure your delivery street address, recipient contact name, active phone number, and notable local landmarks are accurately entered during checkout to prevent fulfillment delays.

Delivery Communication

Our customer concierge team reaches out directly via WhatsApp, telephone, or email to verify delivery fee details and notify you when your parcel has been transferred to dispatch.

Still have questions?

Need assistance with a pending order or want to inquire about custom shipping arrangements? Our concierge team is happy to assist.

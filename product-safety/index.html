Product Safety & Care

Simple guidance for using and caring for your Slimky Hair products.

IMPORTANT GUIDANCE

Supplementary Information: This page offers general cosmetic handling and routine care guidance. Individual product detail pages and product packaging containers remain the primary source for product-specific instructions, ingredient profiles, and precautions.

Essential Protocols

General Product Guidelines

Fundamental care recommendations to help you enjoy your botanical hair-care rituals safely.

Cosmetic Products

Not Medical Treatments

Slimky Hair products are cosmetic personal care products designed to support healthy-looking, manageable hair and scalp aesthetics. They are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition or scalp disorder.

Formulated strictly for aesthetic hair care and grooming.

External Use Only

For external use only.

All Slimky Hair formulations are intended exclusively for topical application to the hair and scalp. Do not ingest or apply internally. Products should always be used according to their individual product instructions.

Keep out of reach of unattended children.

Eye Care

Avoid contact with eyes.

Take care to keep shampoos, oils, and styling formulas away from the eye contour. If accidental contact occurs, follow appropriate product-label guidance and rinse the eyes gently and thoroughly with clean, lukewarm water.

If ocular discomfort persists, seek guidance from an eye care professional.

Scalp Sensitivity

Discontinue use if irritation occurs.

While our products are developed with careful consideration, individual skin sensitivities can occur. If redness, burning, or discomfort develops, discontinue use immediately. Customers can contact Slimky support if they have questions about the product.

Patch testing on a small area before full application is recommended.

Ingredients & INCI

Complete Ingredient Transparency

Customers can find the complete INCI ingredients (International Nomenclature of Cosmetic Ingredients) list for each formulation directly on its individual product page and printed on the container label.

Review full listings if you have known botanical allergies.

Shelf Life & Storage

PAO & Product Care

Shelf-life and Period After Opening (PAO) symbols are provided on individual product listings and packaging containers where available. Store products with lids tightly sealed in a cool, dry place away from direct heat and moisture.

Thoughtful Selection

Choosing Your Products

Selecting the appropriate formulation begins with understanding your unique hair texture and scalp environment. Please review the following before choosing:

01
Hair Type

Consider curl pattern, density, and strand thickness (coily, curly, wavy, or fine).

02
Scalp Type

Assess whether your scalp tends toward dryness, oiliness, or build-up accumulation.

03
Product Type

Clarify your routine goal—cleansing, daily hydration, moisture sealing, or scalp care.

04
Ingredients

Inspect the INCI ingredients list to confirm compatibility with your personal preferences.

05
Instructions

Review product-specific instructions and recommended application frequency.

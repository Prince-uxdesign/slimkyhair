Your Hair Care Guide

Simple, useful guidance for building a thoughtful hair-care routine.

Texture & Structure

Understanding Your Hair Type

Natural hair ranges from fine waves to dense 4C coily textures. Identifying your coil diameter, density, and natural porosity helps you choose between featherweight elixirs or rich, moisture-sealing plant butters.

  • • Fine to medium coils thrive on lightweight hydration and leave-in liquids.
  • • High-density coarse strands benefit from rich cupuaçu and shea butter sealants.
  • • Porosity determines how fast moisture enters and escapes your hair cuticles.

Foundation Science

Understanding Your Scalp Type

A healthy, balanced scalp creates the essential foundation for resilient hair growth. Notice whether your scalp tends to be dry, oil-prone, or sensitive to heavy product buildup.

  • • Dry scalps respond best to soothing botanical oils like castor and jojoba.
  • • Scalps with buildup need periodic clarifying washes with non-stripping botanicals.
  • • Soothe itching with anti-inflammatory botanical extracts and light scalp serums.

Core Regimen

Building a Simple Hair-Care Routine

An intentional routine doesn't require a dozen complicated steps. Focus on three fundamental pillars: purifying without stripping, infusing sustained water hydration, and sealing lipid moisture.

  • • Step 1 (Cleanse): Wash with sulfate-free hydrating cleansers.
  • • Step 2 (Condition): Replenish moisture slip with rich botanical conditioners.
  • • Step 3 (Seal): Lock in moisture with botanical oils or nourishing hair butters.

Botanical Lipids

How to Use Hair Oils

Pure botanical oils seal hydration into hair strands and nourish the scalp barrier. Since oils do not contain water, they should always be layered over water-based leave-in moisturizers.

  • • Apply directly to the scalp with a dropper for targeted follicle nourishment.
  • • Smooth 2-3 drops through damp hair ends to seal cuticles and prevent breakage.
  • • Use as a warm pre-shampoo oil treatment to shield delicate hair from wash fatigue.

Moisture & Slip

How to Use Conditioners

Conditioners soften cuticles and restore elastic flexibility to textured curls. Distinguish between rinse-out treatment detanglers and leave-in moisture shields.

  • • Rinse-out conditioners: Distribute evenly after cleansing, wait 3-5 mins, and rinse.
  • • Leave-in conditioners: Apply generously to damp clean strands for all-day softness.
  • • Deep repair masks: Apply with gentle warmth every 1-2 weeks for intensive strengthening.

Texture Care

Caring for Natural Hair

Natural curls and coils possess unique beauty and delicate points of curvature. Retaining length requires prioritizing moisture retention, minimal tension, and gentle detangling rituals.

  • • Always detangle in sections with conditioner and wide-tooth tools when damp.
  • • Sleep on satin or silk pillowcases to prevent moisture friction and night frizz.
  • • Honor your natural curl pattern without using aggressive chemical straighteners.

Protective Styling

Caring for Protective Styles

Braids, twists, and updos protect delicate hair ends from everyday manipulation. Keeping your scalp hydrated and tension low ensures your natural hair flourishes throughout the wear time.

  • • Keep scalp clean and refreshed using a lightweight soothing scalp serum.
  • • Mist braids or twists with botanical hydrating lotion 2-3 times per week.
  • • Gently protect your perimeter hairline edges with botanical edge balm.

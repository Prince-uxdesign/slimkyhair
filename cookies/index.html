01

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are compact text files placed onto your computer, tablet, or smartphone by websites you visit. They are widely utilized across the web to allow websites to work efficiently, recognize your device across page visits, and remember your selections.

In addition to traditional cookies, we may utilize modern browser storage technologies like localStorage to store your saved items safely on your device without transmitting unnecessary data on every request.

02

How We Use Cookies

We use cookies and storage technologies for clear, practical purposes that directly enhance your browsing and shopping experience:

  • Shopping Bag Retention: Keeping your selected botanical hair oils, shampoos, and conditioners in your bag drawer while you continue browsing.
  • Wishlist Persistence: Remembering the formulations you've saved to your Wishlist so you can revisit them anytime.
  • Account Access: Verifying your session so you don't need to re-enter your password on every page.
  • Performance Optimization: Ensuring our editorial photography and store pages render quickly on mobile and desktop devices.
03

Types of Cookies We Use

Below is a summary of the cookie categories that power the Slimky Hair digital storefront:

Essential Cookies

Session / Up to 1 year

Strictly necessary for core storefront operations, shopping bag drawer persistence, checkout navigation, and account authentication.

Examples: slimky_hair_cart, slimky_active_session, slimky_auth_sessions

Preference Cookies

Up to 1 year

Remembers your personalized choices, such as items saved in your Wishlist, currency preference, and dismissed notification banners.

Examples: slimky_hair_wishlist, currency_pref, banner_dismissed

Functional Cookies

Session

Enables interactive features like drawer transitions, accordion states, and search suggestion caching.

Examples: drawer_state, search_cache

Analytics & Performance

Up to 2 years

Helps us understand aggregated, anonymized visitor patterns to improve page speed, catalog browsing, and mobile layout responsiveness.

Examples: _ga_metrics (aggregated only)
04

Essential Cookies in Detail

Essential cookies are strictly required for the website to provide the commercial services you explicitly request. Without these cookies, services like checkout, shopping bag persistence, and secure session management cannot be provided.

Because these cookies are necessary for the basic operation of the store, they cannot be turned off through our website controls.

05

Analytics & Performance

We may use aggregated analytics tools to understand how visitors engage with our formulations, which categories are explored most frequently, and whether customers encounter any technical errors.

All analytics data is collected in an aggregated, pseudonymous format. We do not use analytics tools to track your browsing across unrelated external websites.

06

Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You have the right to decide whether to accept or decline cookies. You can configure or amend your web browser controls to accept or reject cookies:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies and other site data.
  • Apple Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data.
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions.

Note on Disabling Cookies: If you choose to block or delete all cookies via your browser settings, key interactive parts of our website (such as your shopping cart drawer and wishlist) may not function as intended.

07

Third-Party Cookies & Integrations

Certain third-party service providers—such as payment processors Paystack and Flutterwave—may deploy cookies when you interact with their payment checkout modals to facilitate secure transaction verification and fraud prevention.

These third-party tools operate under their respective privacy and cookie guidelines.

08

Changes to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time in order to reflect changes to the cookies we use or for operational, legal, or regulatory reasons.

Please revisit this Cookie Policy periodically to stay informed about our use of cookies and related technologies.

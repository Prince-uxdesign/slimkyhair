FAQ

Everything You Need to Know

Quick answers to common questions about our products, orders and delivery.

Can I order without creating an account?
Yes. You can complete your purchase using our streamlined guest checkout. If you choose to create an account later, your order history will automatically link using your email address.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major debit and credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Verve), direct bank transfers via Paystack/Flutterwave, and international payments with instant automated verification.
Do you deliver outside Nigeria?
Yes. In addition to nationwide courier delivery across all 36 states in Nigeria, we offer tracked worldwide express shipping through trusted international logistics partners.
How are delivery fees calculated?
Delivery fees are calculated transparently at checkout based on your destination and total parcel weight. Orders meeting our regional threshold qualify for complimentary shipping.
How do I choose the right product?
Our collection is organized by hair texture and routine need—from scalp nourishment to moisture sealing. You can browse by category or take our interactive 2-minute Hair Diagnostic Quiz.
How can I contact Slimky Hair?
Our concierge team is available Monday through Saturday via email at care@slimkyhair.com or direct messaging through our client care line. We are here to guide your routine.
